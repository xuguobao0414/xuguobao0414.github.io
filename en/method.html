Method

How I Work

Fourteen years of police casework and eleven years of defence taught me one habit: every fact must have a source, and every conclusion must be reproducible. Each method below has been tested in live cases.

METHOD 01

Scalpel-Style Document Dissection

Criminal case files and civil materials are dissected in parallel: the same document is read twice — once for its criminal finding, once for its civil entitlement.

  • Criminal–civil conflation: where the same fact is evaluated differently in criminal and civil proceedings;
  • Nested facts: where a large fact contains smaller ones, or a total conceals its components — the trap in how the amount is calculated;
  • Conflicting legal evaluations: under criminal-first or parallel proceedings, the divergence in findings on contract validity, property title and security liability;
  • Openings are pursued in both substance and procedure, producing an integrated plan: criminal defence + asset preservation + civil confirmation of title.

On paper, it comes down to four work products. Dissection is not impression — every layer yields a reviewable deliverable:

Work productWhat is dissectedWhat it is for
Criminal/civil fact comparisonThe same payment or contract, lined up across the criminal file and the civil judgment: wording, evidential source, findingLocating where the two proceedings diverge — the starting point of a defence
Amount breakdownCharged total → components → exclusions (repaid, unrelated, double-counted, handled by others) → admissible amountPushing “especially huge” down a tier, which moves the sentencing band
Procedural conflict listStay of proceedings, transfer of jurisdiction, res judicata, conditions for criminal-first versus parallel proceedingsBuying time and initiative on procedure
Integrated planThe main defence line, timing of asset preservation, sequence of civil claimsGiving the client an executable roadmap, not just a plea

Example: the same sum is recorded as “misappropriated” in the criminal file, but as “consideration for equity” or a “shareholder loan” in the civil judgment. That gap is not a drafting flaw — it is an entry point. Whether it can be used depends on whether the two documents were actually read against each other, page by page.

METHOD 02

Structured Case Files

Every case gets a structured file: each fact bound to an evidence reference and a confirmation status, so that any fact can answer “where does it come from, who confirmed it, and to what extent”.

CategoryFiling ruleRequired elements
Confirmed factNumbered (F001, F002 …)Evidence name, page, confirming person and scope
Statement or leadListed separately; never marked “confirmed”Source and current status
Open questionNumbered list (Q001, Q002 …)Description of the doubt and the verification route
Verified knowledgeMapped to the knowledge baseScope, version, original link, verification date
Working hypothesisAnalysis documents onlyExplicitly labelled “hypothesis”; never mixed into the fact layer

Changing a confirmed fact requires keeping the previous version and the basis for correction — no silent overwriting. That is case-file discipline.

METHOD 03

Technical Evidence Review

Taking audit and appraisal opinions as the model: reviewed layer by layer along procedure — scope — materials — process — reasoning — opinion — application. Failing any one layer is enough to undermine its probative value.

  • Nature of the document: an audit report is not a forensic accounting opinion — the issuing body, the professional basis and the qualifications on file are checked item by item;
  • Material red lines: whether key data come solely from the complainant’s own records, or whether testimonial evidence has been mixed in;
  • Internal consistency: cross-checking text, tables and annexes, going back to the scanned original page for every discrepancy;
  • Method review: whether an overall “balance” can equal an “amount appropriated” — the nature of an account determines the nature of the conclusion;
  • Comparable cases: testing the opposing logic against settled rulings (e.g. “unexplained expenditure is not treated as appropriation”).
METHOD 04

AI-Assisted Casework

Years of practice encoded into instructions an AI can execute, so that AI acts as an operator for file review and fact management, never as the source of conclusions:

  • OCR and text extraction are used only to locate; every key passage is checked against the original;
  • Mechanical checks (arithmetic, date computation, cross-page breaks) are delegated to scripts, and any misread is removed rather than filed;
  • Statutes and authorities are verified against legal databases before being cited, with the source recorded;
  • Every output — fact sheets, chronologies, review opinions — traces back to a page in the file.
