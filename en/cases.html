Suspended & Reduced Sentences

Where the amount is huge and the band starts at three years, winning a result that keeps the client out of prison
Contract fraud → embezzlement (charge changed) · RMB 5.32m · suspended sentence

Mr. D** — embezzlement by taking advantage of office

The case was opened and arrested on a contract-fraud charge. Defence counsel argued that the client had used his position to retain company property, so the conduct should be characterised as embezzlement — an argument that carried the case through indictment and trial on the embezzlement charge. During trial counsel pressed for restitution and reconciliation: the family repaid the full RMB 5.12 million of unlawful gain and prepaid the fine, and the victim company granted forgiveness. He was sentenced to three years' imprisonment, suspended for five years.

◆Charge-based defence (contract fraud → embezzlement) · full restitution and the victim company's forgiveness were both secured through defence work · suspended sentence
Smuggling of ordinary goods · tax evaded RMB 3.19m · suspended sentence

Mr. Z** — smuggling of ordinary goods

Prosecuted as the person directly in charge of the corporate entity, with customs calculating evaded tax at RMB 3.19 million; the client was arrested before transfer for prosecution. Defence counsel challenged the valuation methodology and the amount assessed, and built the mitigation case on a plea of guilty, acceptance of punishment and voluntary disgorgement. After the client paid RMB 500,000 of unlawful gain to the court and the company prepaid the full fine, both the sentencing recommendation and the defence submissions were adopted: three years' imprisonment, suspended for five years. The objection to the valuation method for this category of goods was subsequently absorbed into judicial interpretation, and the amounts charged in such cases fell sharply thereafter.

◆Valuation-method challenge (later absorbed into judicial interpretation) · guilty plea and acceptance of punishment · RMB 500,000 disgorged · suspended sentence despite pre-indictment arrest

Contract fraud · only suspended sentence among 22 defendants

Mr. L* — contract fraud (antique auction scheme)

A classic “antique auction” contract-fraud prosecution with 22 co-defendants. The investigation characterised the client as a shareholder of the company. Defence counsel checked the company registration, capital contributions and dividend records, and compared the co-defendants' statements (which conflicted on the origin and size of the shareholding), establishing that the client was merely a business director, not a shareholder, so his liability should not be assessed on a shareholder footing. Counsel also persuaded the court to set a model approach for later asset recovery in the case. He received a suspended sentence — the only one among all 22 defendants.

◆Shareholder-status defence (shareholder → business director) · the only suspended sentence among 22 co-defendants
